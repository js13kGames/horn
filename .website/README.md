The last unicorn. One concrete pit. A whole lot of machines.

Survive 60 seconds, then destroy the Warden to escape. Aim your horn, hold fire, collect upgrades from the ground, and turn rainbow drops into six seconds of overdrive. Dash through danger and chain kills to get your dash back.

**Controls**
- WASD / arrows: move
- Mouse: aim; hold left click: fire
- Space / Shift: dash
- P / Escape: pause
- R: restart; M: sound; F: fullscreen

Start with five hearts. Pick up more to reach seven. Defeat the boss to win.
